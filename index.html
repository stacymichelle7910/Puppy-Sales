Puppy Training Tips for Beginners

  • Start early & be consistent: Begin training the day you bring your puppy home. Use the same commands and rewards every time.
  • Use positive reinforcement: Reward good behavior with treats, praise, or play — never punish mistakes.
  • Keep sessions short: 5-10 minutes, 2-3 times a day. Puppies have short attention spans.
  • Focus on basics first: Teach "sit", "stay", "come", and name response before anything else.
  • Potty training: Take them out after meals, naps, and play. Praise immediately after they go outside.
  • Socialize safely: Expose to new people, sounds, and environments gradually after vaccinations.
  • Crate training: Make the crate a positive, safe space — never use it for punishment.

Patience is key — every puppy learns at their own pace!
